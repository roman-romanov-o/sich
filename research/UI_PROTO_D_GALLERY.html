Five directions for Sich

Each is a working prototype: click a screenshot to open it, then use the Simulate panel in its corner (Agent asks · Answer · Quota low · Theme). All five keep one rule: no colour until something needs you.

1 · Lanes timeline, not a kanban

Every session is a row with a three-hour track of its history ending at now. You can see who has been stalling and how long each wait lasted. Quota lives in the toolbar.

one needs you calm dark

2 · Exposé the board is the terminals

A Mission Control grid of live terminal thumbnails. The one that needs you comes forward, full-colour, with its question as a sheet; the rest recede by size and depth. Click a thumbnail to zoom it to the window.

one needs you calm dark

3 · Native a first-party Mac app

Three panes like Mail or Xcode: a translucent source list grouped by state, the selected session's terminal in the middle, quota and session details in an inspector. A permission request is a sheet dropping from the toolbar.

one needs you calm dark

4 · Wall a status board for a second monitor

Readable from across the room: one huge number says how many need you, then one tall row per session with clocks ticking by the second, and quota as big percentages. Nothing to click except Allow and Deny.

one needs you calm light

5 · Inbox no board at all

A single prioritised list like Things or Reminders. What needs you sits expanded at the top and gets checked off when answered; working sessions are one line each; everything else is folded away.
