Sich — first launch on a new Mac
================================

1. Drag "Sich" onto the Applications folder in this window.

2. macOS will refuse to open it the first time ("Apple could not verify Sich is
   free of malware"). That is expected: this build is ad-hoc signed, not
   notarized with an Apple Developer ID. Nothing is wrong with the app.

   Easiest way through — open Terminal and run:

       xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Sich.app

   Then open Sich normally.

   Or, without the Terminal: try to open Sich once, let it be blocked, then go to
   System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll to Security, and click
   "Open Anyway" next to the message about Sich. Confirm on the next prompt.

Requirements
------------
* macOS 14 (Sonoma) or newer. Apple Silicon and Intel are both supported.
* The agent CLIs you intend to drive — e.g. Claude Code (`claude`) or Codex —
  installed and already logged in. Sich watches and hosts your sessions; it does
  not ship or authenticate them for you.

What it changes on your machine
-------------------------------
Sich reads agent status from lifecycle hooks, so on launch it writes:

* ~/.sich/            — a self-contained hook forwarder script and PATH shims
* ~/.claude/settings.json — adds its forwarder to the hook events it watches
                            (existing hooks of yours are preserved, not replaced)

Sessions run under your own interactive login. Sich never launches `claude -p`,
so it cannot spend your API credit behind your back.
